FROM OUR KITCHEN

A cookbook that grows with us.

Five family-approved dish choices, complete recipes, and a little less thinking at dinner time.

A few useful things

  • Shopping ticks, edits, portions and favourites save in this browser. They do not sync between phones. Export a list to share it.
  • Aisles follow a typical Aldi-style shop; your store may be laid out differently. Amounts are recipe quantities, not rounded pack sizes. Check your pantry and round up whole produce and packs.
  • Nutrition is an approximate recipe-level estimate per equal serving at the recipe’s original yield. Brands, swaps, oil absorption and portions change it; it is not a personalised target for children.
  • Allergen notes are prompts, not a guarantee. Check every product label and manage cross-contact.
  • Recipe methods were written for this site and have not been kitchen-tested by the family yet.
  • The homepage features a playful cartoon rendering of Anthony and Kym. Recipe photos are illustrative and may differ from the exact finished recipe.
