Rear brake pads & rotors

2013 Volkswagen Touareg 3.0L V6 TDI · Both rear wheels · A guided, tick-as-you-go replacement for Dad.

Pads + rotors onlyVCDS availableNo parking brake shoe replacement
Read before startingThis is a paraphrased guide based on VW-derived service information and Ross-Tech, not an official VW publication or a substitute for VIN-specific factory instructions. Brakes are safety-critical. Verify your brake PR code, parts and fastener specifications, use axle stands, and stop if hardware or measurements differ. Do not drive until the hydraulic brakes and parking brake have been confirmed safe.

    Tools & critical specifications

    Tools and parts
    • Correct rear pads and matching rear rotors for the car's VIN/PR code; wear sensor where needed
    • Four new rear caliper mounting bolts (single-use)
    • Jack, correctly rated axle stands, wheel chocks; air-suspension jack mode if fitted
    • Appropriate sockets/drive bits, torque wrench and angle gauge
    • VW T10145-equivalent brake piston compression tool, caliper hanger
    • Brake cleaner, hub cleaning brush, brake fluid extraction tool, gloves and eye protection
    • VCDS to save initial scan and check final parking brake gap
    Fastener specifications — verify by VIN
    • Rear caliper-to-wheel-bearing-housing: 30 Nm + 75°, new bolts.
    • Rotor retaining screw: 15 Nm.
    • Wheel bolts: 180 Nm (verify wheel/vehicle specification).
    • Parking brake air gap: 1.9–2.4 mm; difference between sides no more than 0.2 mm.

    The VW 2013 quick-reference specification lists caliper and rotor torque figures. Dimensions and wear limits vary by brake package.
